## Selected Research & Projects

**Applied research. Practical questions. Data-driven solutions.**

</div>

<br>

### Rainfall–NDVI & Vegetation Analysis

Geospatial and remote-sensing research examining relationships among rainfall,
vegetation dynamics, and environmental conditions.

**Methods:** `GIS` · `NDVI` · `Remote Sensing` · `Environmental Analytics`

<br>

### Energy-Aware & Green Scheduling

Computational research focused on improving performance while reducing
energy consumption and environmental impact.

**Methods:** `Green Computing` · `HPC` · `Optimization`

<br>

### Mobile Network Performance — The Gambia

Quantitative and geospatial analysis of cellular network performance,
regional connectivity, and telecommunications infrastructure.

**Methods:** `Telecommunications` · `Network Analytics` · `GIS`

<br>

### Institutional Solar PV Load Analysis

Data-driven assessment of institutional energy requirements and opportunities
for sustainable photovoltaic infrastructure.

**Methods:** `Solar PV` · `Energy Analytics` · `Sustainability`

<br>

### Biogas Resource Assessment — The Gambia

Investigation of organic resources and their potential contribution
to renewable-energy development.

**Methods:** `Biogas` · `Renewable Energy` · `Resource Assessment`

<br>

### Improved Indirect Solar Mango Dryer

Applied renewable-energy research combining solar technology,
agriculture, engineering, and sustainable post-harvest preservation.

**Methods:** `Solar Energy` · `Agriculture` · `Applied Research`

<br>

### GIS Land-Use / Land-Cover Analysis

Application of geospatial techniques for understanding land-use patterns,
environmental conditions, and geographic change.

**Methods:** `GIS` · `Spatial Analysis` · `Remote Sensing`

<br>

### Machine Learning & Data Analytics

Application of statistical and machine-learning approaches
to interdisciplinary research problems and real-world datasets.

**Methods:** `Machine Learning` · `Data Science` · `Analytics`
