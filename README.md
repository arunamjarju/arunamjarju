### Researcher · Data Scientist · Educator

Dr. Aruna M. Jarju works at the intersection of **computational intelligence, data science, geospatial technologies, sustainable computing, renewable energy, telecommunications, and applied research**.

His research focuses on developing **data-driven and computational solutions** for real-world challenges involving energy efficiency, environmental analysis, telecommunications, intelligent systems, sustainable infrastructure, and technology-enabled development.

He serves as **Director of KGS – Academic Support Services at Monroe University**, where his work supports academic research, quantitative analysis, interdisciplinary scholarship, technology education, and research development.

His broader research agenda emphasizes the responsible application of technology to challenges with meaningful **academic, environmental, institutional, and societal impact**.

# Selected Research & Projects

**Applied Research · Computational Methods · Sustainable Impact**

</div>

<br>


## 01 — Rainfall–NDVI & Vegetation Analysis

Geospatial and remote-sensing research examining relationships between **rainfall variability, vegetation dynamics, and environmental conditions**.

**Research Methods**

`GIS` · `NDVI` · `Remote Sensing` · `Environmental Analytics`

---

## 02 — Energy-Aware & Green Scheduling

Computational research focused on improving system performance while reducing **energy consumption and environmental impact**.

**Research Methods**

`Green Computing` · `HPC` · `Optimization` · `Energy Efficiency`

---

## 03 — Mobile Network Performance — The Gambia

Quantitative and geospatial investigation of **cellular network performance, regional connectivity, and telecommunications infrastructure**.

**Research Methods**

`Telecommunications` · `GIS` · `Network Analytics`

---

## 04 — Institutional Solar PV Load Analysis

Assessment of institutional energy requirements and opportunities for **sustainable photovoltaic infrastructure and energy optimization**.

**Research Methods**

`Solar PV` · `Energy Analytics` · `Sustainability`

---

## 05 — Biogas Resource Assessment — The Gambia

Investigation of organic resources and their potential contribution to **renewable-energy development and sustainable resource utilization**.

**Research Methods**

`Biogas` · `Renewable Energy` · `Resource Assessment`

---

## 06 — Improved Indirect Solar Mango Dryer

Applied renewable-energy research combining **solar technology, agricultural systems, engineering, and sustainable post-harvest preservation**.

**Research Methods**

`Solar Energy` · `Agriculture` · `Applied Research`

---

## 07 — GIS Land-Use / Land-Cover Analysis

Application of geospatial methods for understanding **land-use patterns, environmental conditions, and geographic change**.

**Research Methods**

`GIS` · `Spatial Analysis` · `Remote Sensing`

---

## 08 — Machine Learning & Data Analytics

Application of machine-learning and statistical approaches to **interdisciplinary datasets and real-world research problems**.

**Research Methods**

`Machine Learning` · `Data Science` · `Analytics`
